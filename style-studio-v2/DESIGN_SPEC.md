# Style Studio V2 — Visual & UX Specification

## Objective
Make the existing Style Studio feel like a premium digital atelier consultation that produces a useful Style Brief and encourages exploration of Jane.M collections, promotions and WhatsApp consultation.

## Visual source of truth
Use `REFERENCE_MOCKUP.png`. Match the editorial hierarchy, warm ivory base, oxblood/burgundy, muted gold, serif display typography, fine couture icons, whitespace, subtle rules and restrained shadows. Do not turn it into a SaaS dashboard.

## Journey
Present six understandable stages where compatible with existing logic: Occasion, Style, Design details, Measurements, Inspiration, Review & Brief. Do not duplicate existing questions.

## Contextual Jane.M discovery
At relevant moments surface real Jane.M looks from the repository, e.g. graduation looks after Graduation is selected. Never invent a garment and present it as Jane.M work. Preserve the user's progress when they explore collection content.

## Result hierarchy
1. Style direction
2. At-a-glance attributes
3. Why it works
4. Measurements if supplied
5. Inspiration/reference content
6. Indicative workmanship/budget guidance
7. To confirm with Jane.M

Primary CTA: `Discuss My Look With Jane.M`
Secondary: `Download Brief (PDF)` and `Share My Brief`.

## Promotion
Show current promotion elegantly in hero/result areas, sourced from current repo/config. Do not repeat it excessively.

## Recommended content
After results, show `You might also like` using only real Jane.M work already available in the repository.

## Dark mode
Use warm charcoal, burgundy, warm ivory and readable gold. Preserve contrast and live theme switching.

## Mobile
Recompose into a deliberate single-column journey. Do not squeeze the desktop layout.
