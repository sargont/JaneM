# Jane.M Atelier competitor analysis

**Purpose:** Define where Jane.M can win in Maseru, Lesotho and selected South African demand without copying competitors.

## The opportunity

Jane.M should own **designer-led, made-to-measure occasionwear**: wedding, bridesmaid, graduation, gala and evening looks, corsetry and custom womenswear. The differentiator is not merely a dress; it is the process from consultation and measurements to fittings and a final piece made for one person and one occasion.

The market divides into four buyer needs:

| Buyer need | Competitors seen | What Jane.M should lead with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready-to-wear and a visible product catalogue | Thethana, H.MOR | Jane.M is for a look made around the client, rather than off-the-rack choice. |
| Bridal showroom or rental | The Bridal House Lesotho | Original design, private measurements, fit and a gown that belongs to the bride. |
| Designer fashion and runway credibility | Kima D and other local designers | Jane.M's documented designer experience, fashion-house background, corsetry teaching and JaneM TV audience. |
| Long-tail Google search pages aimed at Maseru | Foxxy Brides, D&D Clothing | Real local portfolio evidence, accurate local Business Profile, useful occasion pages and client reviews. |

## Competitor readout

### Thethana — retail, heritage and collections

[Thethana](https://thethana.com/pages/about-us) presents a fashion and lifestyle brand rooted in Lesotho, built around intentional design, heritage, versatility and collections. Its [company profile](https://ls.linkedin.com/company/thethana) describes retail apparel, manufacturing and African-inspired fashion/home styling.

**Strengths:** a coherent heritage story, collections, product-led shopping and a broader lifestyle proposition.

**Jane.M response:** do not compete by becoming another heritage retail label. Keep a more intimate premium position: *Wear Jane.M for the occasion that matters to you.* Show the consultation, design sketch, craftsmanship, fit and client outcome. Thethana’s strength leaves room for an atelier that starts with the individual woman.

### Kima D — runway association, limited public search footprint

The clearest independently discoverable record is a [Lesotho Fashion Week AW23 showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgbq1kOiGaM), featuring a ten-piece womenswear collection.

**Strengths:** local runway credibility and creative fashion positioning.

**Gap visible in search:** there is little independently discoverable web information compared with an owned website, organised occasion pages, a verified Business Profile and a clear customer journey. This is a search observation, not a claim about the scale or quality of the business.

**Jane.M response:** make the work easy to find and easy to trust. Publish a small number of real portfolio stories with final looks, captions, the occasion, a client-approved quote and a clear booking action. Jane.M’s existing designer credentials and JaneM TV audience are proof points that should appear consistently on the designer page, YouTube descriptions, Google Business posts and social profiles.

### The Bridal House Lesotho — selection and appointment experience

[The Bridal House Lesotho](https://www.evepla.com/LS/Maseru/109790960614321/The-Bridal-House-Lesotho) presents a bridal-shop model focused on booked fittings, a premium gown collection and rentals/showroom choice.

**Strengths:** product selection, an established bridal appointment experience and a dedicated bridal focus.

**Jane.M response:** offer what a showroom cannot: a gown created from the client’s body, taste, comfort requirements and wedding story. The wedding page should show a clear 4-step commission process, planning lead times, a real fitting image and three to five finished bridal examples. It should never claim that all timelines or styles are available.

### H.MOR — ready-to-wear product clarity

[H.MOR](https://www.hmorstores.com/) has product pages with images, prices, sizes, a Maseru boutique location and WhatsApp contact.

**Strengths:** instant browsing, price anchors, size availability and simple purchase intent.

**Jane.M response:** do not hide behind vague luxury language. Give custom clients equivalent clarity: what a consultation covers, how deposits work, what affects workmanship cost, what happens during fittings and how early to book. This reduces uncertainty without turning bespoke work into an off-the-shelf catalogue.

### Foxxy Brides and D&D Clothing — search-first Maseru pages

[Foxxy Brides](https://foxxy.co.za/gowns/graduation-dresses/maseru) has a detailed Maseru graduation page covering remote fitting and lead time. [D&D Clothing](https://danddclothing.com/en-ca/pages/evening-dress-in-lesotho) also targets occasionwear searches for Lesotho and Maseru.

**Strengths:** they have made pages around the exact queries customers type: graduation dresses in Maseru, evening dresses in Lesotho and custom wedding dresses.

**Jane.M response:** Jane.M already has more credible local foundations: a Maseru atelier, a connected Google Business Profile, WearJaneM.com and real local work. Build deeper pages only where they reflect real services and show real examples. Better local proof will beat generic keyword repetition.

## Positioning to use consistently

> **Jane.M Atelier creates made-to-measure women’s occasionwear in Maseru: wedding and bridesmaid dresses, graduation looks, corsetry, evening gowns and custom pieces, refined through a personal consultation and fitting process.**

Keep **Jane.M Atelier** as the business and atelier name. Use **Wear Jane.M** as the customer-facing expression and domain. Do not force “Wear Jane.M” into every title; natural language is better for people and Google.

## Highest-priority actions

1. **Consolidate the domain.** The site’s `CNAME`, canonicals, sitemap and robots file already point to `wearjanem.com`. Google still showing `sargont.github.io/JaneM/` is legacy indexing. Continue requesting indexing for priority Wear Jane.M pages and use only `wearjanem.com` in every new public profile, video description and link. Do not publish the old GitHub URL again.
2. **Build proof, not filler.** Add one approved portfolio story each month: bridal, graduation, evening/gala or custom womenswear. Include photographs, specific descriptive alt text, the client’s approved story and a booking link.
3. **Turn the Business Profile into local proof.** Add real service entries, post current work, answer reviews and ask completed clients for honest Google reviews. Google says local ranking depends mainly on relevance, distance and prominence; it cannot be bought or requested as a ranking favour. [Google’s guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)
4. **Make consultation certainty visible.** Add the actual lead-time guidance, deposit policy, number of fittings normally planned and what a client should bring. Update this whenever operations change.
5. **Connect every channel.** WearJaneM.com should appear in JaneM TV video descriptions, Instagram bio, Facebook About section, WhatsApp Business profile/catalogue and Google Business Profile. Use tagged URLs for campaign traffic.
6. **Review query data monthly.** In Search Console, track impressions/clicks for: `wedding dresses maseru`, `graduation dresses maseru`, `evening dresses lesotho`, `fashion designer maseru`, `corset dress lesotho`, `bridesmaid dresses lesotho`, and branded searches. Create new pages only after a genuine service and useful content exist.
