# Local SEO checklist

- Keep the business name, country, service area, telephone, WhatsApp, email, hours and Google Business Profile URL current in Content Studio.
- Do not publish a street address until it is verified for customer visits.
- Claim and verify the Google Business Profile before adding its URL.
- Use consistent name, phone and email on the website and Google Business Profile.
- Add new professional photos and complete service descriptions to the verified profile.
