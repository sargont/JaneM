# Content strategy

The journal drafts live outside the deployed `JaneM_Website/` directory in `content/journal-drafts.json` and are intentionally not in the sitemap. Review each draft with Jane.M, add a real author, date, reviewed hero image and useful body copy, then promote it to an indexable article with `Article` JSON-LD.

Prioritise the six drafted topics around graduation dress fit, ordering lead time, graduation-gown underlayers, measurements, custom-dress cost planning and graduation photography. Link each completed article to a relevant collection look and booking CTA; avoid publishing generic, keyword-led copy.
