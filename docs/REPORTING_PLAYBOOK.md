# Jane.M performance reporting

Review these reports every Monday for the previous 28 days. The goal is to understand whether Jane.M is reaching local people, helping them find a relevant dress direction and generating conversations with the atelier.

## Google Analytics: `Jane.M lead actions`

The saved Analytics report filters for customer-intent actions:

- `booking_start` — a visitor chose to start a custom brief
- `consultation_request_prepared` — a visitor completed a consultation request ready for WhatsApp
- `whatsapp_click` — a visitor opened a WhatsApp conversation
- `style_studio_brief_complete` and `style_studio_whatsapp_click` — a visitor reached the Style Studio handoff
- `catalogue_download` and `email_click` — supporting consideration actions

Review it alongside **Landing page** and **Traffic acquisition**. A page or source is working when it creates lead actions, not merely views.

## Google Search Console: saved review setup

Search Console does not provide named, saved custom reports like Analytics. In the `wearjanem.com` property, use **Performance → Search results** with these repeatable views and export them monthly:

1. **Local discovery** — Country: Lesotho; pages: `/`, `/graduation-dresses-lesotho/`, `/wedding-dresses-lesotho/`, `/evening-gowns/`.
2. **South Africa reach** — Country: South Africa; inspect the same pages plus `/south-africa-custom-dresses/`.
3. **Occasion demand** — Query filters containing `graduation`, `wedding`, `bridal`, `gala`, `evening`, `corset`, `dress`, `Maseru`, or `Lesotho`.
4. **Growth and indexing** — Pages report for indexed URLs, exclusions and any new crawl issues.

Record clicks, impressions, CTR and average position. Compare the last 28 days with the preceding 28 days. Do not judge a new page before it has had at least four weeks to be crawled and shown.

## Campaign links

Use tagged links whenever a post or advert points to the site. Example:

`https://wearjanem.com/graduation-dresses-lesotho/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic-social&utm_campaign=graduation-2026`

Use `facebook`, `instagram`, `youtube`, `whatsapp`, `google-business-profile` or `partner` as the source; use `organic-social`, `organic-video`, `status`, `organic-local` or `referral` as the medium; and use a short campaign name such as `graduation-2026`, `bridal-2027`, `profile` or `consultation`.

The site records campaign landings without collecting visitor names, messages or form answers.
