Discuss my look

Jane.M Atelier is Liteboho Mokhethi’s made-to-measure fashion studio in Maseru, Lesotho, for women who want their milestone look to feel personal, refined and memorable. Meet Liteboho through the dedicated designer profile in the main menu.

The atelier is where a client’s occasion, comfort, references and personal style become a considered garment. From graduation dresses and corsetry to wedding and evening looks, the design process is shaped around the person wearing it.
