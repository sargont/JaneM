Simple campaign rules

Everything you need to know.

What qualifies as an entry?

A public Facebook or Instagram post showing you wearing a Jane.M garment, with your Jane.M appreciation card included in the post or carousel, Jane.M tagged, #WearJaneM used and wearjanem.com included in the caption. The post link must be registered with Jane.M on WhatsApp.

How many posts can count?

A maximum of three qualifying posts per entrant can be counted. Jane.M combines the visible likes/reactions from those registered posts into one final score.

What engagement counts?

Only visible Facebook reactions and Instagram likes count, at one point each. Comments, shares, reposts, saves and views do not count toward the competition score.

When does voting close?

The scoring window closes at 23:59 CAT on 18 December 2026. Winners are verified on 19 December and announced on Jane.M social pages on 20 December 2026.

What keeps the competition fair?

Posts must remain public through the winner announcement. Purchased, automated, exchanged or otherwise artificial engagement may result in disqualification. Jane.M may verify public engagement before confirming winners.

Can Jane.M repost my entry?

By entering, you allow Jane.M to repost or feature your submitted campaign content with credit for Jane.M campaign promotion. You retain ownership of your original content.

Anything else?

Jane.M team members directly administering the campaign are not eligible for prizes. This promotion is organised by Jane.M and is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Facebook, Instagram or Meta.
