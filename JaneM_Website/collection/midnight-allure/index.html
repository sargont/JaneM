Discuss this design

Midnight Allure is a statement evening-gown direction with sheer corsetry, refined draping and a confident navy finish. It is designed as inspiration for a made-to-measure conversation, not an off-the-rack item.

Jane.M Atelier helps you decide which elements support your occasion, comfort and preferred level of detail. The draping, coverage, colour, neckline and finish can each be adapted to your event and measurements.

For an evening gown consultation in Maseru, start with your date, preferred mood and reference details. Fabric is selected separately to suit your design and budget, then fittings guide the final finish.
