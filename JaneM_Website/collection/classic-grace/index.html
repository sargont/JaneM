Classic Grace balances dramatic sleeves with a refined satin skirt and structured corsetry. The silhouette is a starting point for a custom graduation, wedding-guest or special-occasion design by Jane.M Atelier.

Your consultation is the place to adjust the length, sleeve volume, neckline and colour. Those choices help the final dress feel considered for your event while supporting your comfort and preferred level of detail.

Use this look as a reference, then send your event date and any inspiration through WhatsApp or Style Studio. Jane.M confirms measurements, fabric and fittings before the final construction direction is set.
