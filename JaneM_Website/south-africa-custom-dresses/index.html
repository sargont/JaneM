Jane.M Atelier offers remote custom-dress consultations for clients in South Africa, with travel considered for larger projects where needed.

Discuss my look

Jane.M Atelier is based in Maseru, Lesotho and works with South African clients through a considered remote consultation process. You can begin from wherever you are by sharing your event date, inspiration, preferred silhouette and the details that matter most to you.

The early design conversation can happen remotely by WhatsApp. Jane.M helps refine the direction, colour, coverage, construction details, fabric plan and quotation before the fitting plan is agreed. This works well for graduation dresses, wedding looks, gala-dinner gowns and other special-occasion pieces.

For a large project, Jane.M may travel to the client’s location when that is necessary and practical. Travel, measurements, fittings, delivery and the production timeline are agreed individually before work is confirmed. This is not a permanent South African studio, so availability is always discussed first.

Consultation notes

Questions, answered.

Can I start a Jane.M consultation from South Africa?+

Yes. The initial custom-dress conversation can happen remotely through WhatsApp, using your event date, inspiration and preferred style direction.

Does Jane.M travel to clients in South Africa?+

For a large project, Jane.M may travel to a client’s location when necessary and practical. Availability, travel and fitting arrangements are agreed before work is confirmed.

How are fittings handled for a South African client?+

The fitting plan is agreed individually once the design, timeline and location are understood. Jane.M confirms the practical details before production begins.

What should I send for a remote consultation?+

Start with your event date, inspiration, preferred silhouette, colour direction and any notes about fit, comfort or coverage.
