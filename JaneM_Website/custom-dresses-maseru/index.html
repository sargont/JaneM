Jane.M Atelier creates custom, made-to-measure women’s fashion in Maseru for graduations, celebrations and meaningful occasions.

Book a consultation

Jane.M Atelier creates made-to-measure women’s fashion in Maseru, Lesotho. A custom dress begins with your occasion, your references and the way you want to feel—not a fixed template.

Start with an inspiration image, a collection direction or the Style Studio. At consultation, Jane.M refines the silhouette, neckline, length, colour, practical needs and finishing details. Measurements are taken and verified before the fitting plan and final construction direction are confirmed.

Fabric is selected separately to suit your design and budget. You may bring a fabric you love, or Jane.M can help you choose one that supports the look. The final quotation follows the confirmed design, measurements and construction review, then fittings and finishing bring the garment together.

Consultation notes

Questions, answered.

Do you create dresses from scratch?+

Jane.M develops a made-to-measure direction around your occasion, preferences, measurements and agreed details.

Can I bring my own inspiration photo?+

Yes. You can bring an inspiration image or attach it in WhatsApp when you send your Style Studio brief.

How are measurements handled?+

Measurements are discussed, taken and verified as part of the consultation and fitting process before production.

How do I book a consultation?+

Use the booking page or WhatsApp Jane.M with your event date, preferred style and any references.
