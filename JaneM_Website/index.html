Made-to-measure fashion in Maseru

Custom dresses for weddings, graduations and special occasions.

Jane.M Atelier creates bespoke dresses in Maseru for women who want a personal, well-fitted look for an important moment. The studio works on custom wedding dress design, graduation dresses, bridesmaid dresses, evening gowns, corsetry, occasionwear, matric dance dresses and custom women’s wear.

Clients looking for a dressmaker in Maseru can start with a reference image, a Jane.M collection look or a Style Studio brief. Jane.M then refines the silhouette, fabric direction, measurements, fitting plan and workmanship quote around the event, comfort needs and preferred level of detail.

The atelier also creates traditional Seshoeshoe-inspired dresses, mother-of-the-bride dresses, church dresses, birthday dresses and gala dinner looks for clients who want clothing designed around their body, occasion and personal style.

The atelier is based at Sekamaneng, Ha Foso 100, Maseru, Lesotho and supports remote consultations for clients in Lesotho and South Africa. Large projects can be discussed with travel where practical.
