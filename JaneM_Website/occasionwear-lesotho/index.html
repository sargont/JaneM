Discuss my look

Many clients do not search by one dress category. They search by the event: birthday dress, church outfit, gala dinner dress, mother of the bride dress or special occasion dress. Jane.M Atelier brings those needs into one made-to-measure occasionwear consultation.

The process considers dress code, comfort, photography, movement, coverage, fit and the level of detail you want. The final garment can be soft and elegant, sculpted and corseted, traditional-inspired or dramatic enough for a formal evening.

Bring the date, venue, dress-code notes, colour direction and reference images. Jane.M confirms the construction approach, fabric plan, workmanship quote, measurements and fitting schedule before production.
