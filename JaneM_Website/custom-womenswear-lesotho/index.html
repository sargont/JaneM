Made-to-measure women’s wear in Lesotho by Jane.M Atelier, from corsetry and evening looks to graduation, wedding guest and personal occasion dressing.

Discuss my look

Jane.M Atelier creates custom women’s wear in Lesotho for clients who want more than a ready-made dress. The work is designer-led, measured, fitted and shaped around the occasion, the wearer and the feeling the garment should carry.

This includes made-to-measure graduation dresses, corset dresses, evening gowns, wedding guest outfits, bridesmaid dresses, matric dance dresses, mother of the bride dresses, birthday dresses, church dresses, gala dinner looks and other special-occasion pieces. Jane.M can also discuss a personal custom piece when the brief, timeline and construction direction are clear.

The process starts with a conversation. Bring your occasion, event date, reference images, comfort needs, colour direction and budget range. Jane.M confirms the design, fabric plan, workmanship quotation, measurements and fitting approach before production begins.

Consultation notes

Questions, answered.

What custom women’s wear does Jane.M make?+

Jane.M focuses on made-to-measure occasionwear such as graduation dresses, corsetry, evening gowns, bridesmaid dresses, wedding guest looks, matric dance dresses, mother of the bride looks, birthday dresses, church dresses, gala dinner dresses and other personal custom pieces.

Is this a retail clothing store?+

No. Jane.M is a made-to-measure atelier. Designs are discussed, measured, quoted and fitted around the client rather than sold as fixed off-the-rack stock.

Can Jane.M help if I only have a general idea?+

Yes. You can begin with a mood, event date or reference image, then refine the design direction through Style Studio or consultation.
