How to play Pattern Drop
  1. Move and rotate each fabric piece. The dotted outline previews its landing.
  2. Fill every square in a row to stitch it away. Clearing several rows at once scores more.
  3. Clear rows with consecutive pieces for a chain bonus. Eight rows complete one illustrated look.
  4. Deliver all three looks at 24 rows. If the fabric reaches the top, try another run.

Today’s fabric order stays the same on each retry. A new mix arrives at midnight in Lesotho and South Africa. Classic speeds up every four rows; Relaxed keeps a steady pace.

On a phone, tap the controls or hold Left, Right or Lower. Place drops the piece immediately. Sound begins when you play; mute it whenever you like.

Best scores stay in this browser, separately for each pace. Clearing site data removes them. A run ends when you leave this page.
