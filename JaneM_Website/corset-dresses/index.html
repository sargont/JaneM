Explore custom-made corset dresses by Jane.M Atelier in Lesotho, tailored around your occasion, fit and preferred finish.

Book a consultation

Corsetry can bring shape, support and a strong point of view to a custom dress. Jane.M Atelier uses corset-inspired structure in ways that can feel refined, soft, dramatic or minimal, depending on your occasion and comfort preferences.

At consultation, the neckline, coverage, skirt balance and support are considered together. A corset direction can sit within a graduation dress, evening gown or occasion look; the goal is a considered silhouette that feels secure and wearable.

Your measurements and fitting needs guide the final direction. Fabric is selected separately to suit your design and budget, then Jane.M confirms the construction and workmanship quotation before production.
