Made-to-measure wedding guest and occasion dresses by Jane.M Atelier for celebrations where you want to feel confidently yourself.

Book a consultation

For a wedding, birthday, dinner or meaningful celebration, the right dress should work with the atmosphere while still feeling unmistakably yours. Jane.M Atelier turns your occasion, dress code and personal style into a made-to-measure direction.

A consultation can explore length, colour, sleeves, coverage, movement and the focal point you want the garment to have. Bring a reference, a collection look or simply a feeling you want to capture.

Measurements and fitting needs help shape the final direction. Fabric is selected separately to suit your design and budget, and Jane.M confirms the workmanship quotation once the design and construction approach are clear.
