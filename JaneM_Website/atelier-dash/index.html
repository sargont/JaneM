How to play
  1. You’re the golden spool. Steer through the workroom; your next turn is queued.
  2. Gather the little gold stitches and four marked supplies. Your dress takes shape here.
  3. A blue thimble gives six seconds of protection from the scissors.
  4. Once everything is collected, return to the centre mannequin. Complete three rounds with three lives.

Pause anytime. Each new round brings a faster chase. Practice mode slows the scissors; it has its own best score.
